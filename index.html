Biography

Jinpeng Yu (于劲鹏) is currently the Content Generation Algorithm Lead at Qwen App, Alibaba Group, where he focuses on multimodal AI agents and generative models for image and video content creation. He previously worked on the AIGC team at Xiaohongshu, focusing on controllable and conditional image generation as well as multimodal understanding and generation. He received his bachelor's degree with honors from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) in 2021. Subsequently, he earned his master's degree from SIST, ShanghaiTech University in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Shenghua Gao (高盛华, HKU).
